Frequently asked questions

What is the average human reaction time on a visual test?

The standard visual reaction time for healthy adults averages around 200ms to 270ms. Audio reaction speeds are usually faster (140–160ms), whereas touch responses average 150ms.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method using timed intervals—typically 25 minutes of deep focus followed by a 5-minute break—to sustain concentration and prevent mental fatigue.

Are my scores and sound settings saved privacy-first?

Yes. Reaction records, high scores and sound volume preferences are stored only on your device using HTML5 localStorage. No personal data is transmitted anywhere.

How frequently should I practice breathing or focus exercises?

Practicing 3–5 minutes of guided box breathing or Schulte grid training once or twice a day is a reasonable routine for focus and stress management.

Is Calm & Focus free, and do I need an account?

It is completely free. There is no sign-up, login or app download; every tool runs in your browser.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The site is built mobile-first, with touch-friendly controls that adapt to any screen size, in dark or light mode.
